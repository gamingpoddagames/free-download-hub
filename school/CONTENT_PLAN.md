# පුංචි නැණ මඟ — Content & growth plan

## Publishing standard
- All material must be original or explicitly licensed; official exam-style practice is not an official past paper.
- Review Sinhala spelling, grade-level accuracy, correct answers and explanations before posting.
- Do not collect student names, phone numbers or results.
- Target parents and teachers in promotional copy; avoid manipulative or child-directed advertising.

## Weekly content plan
Monday: Grade 1 printable worksheet + answer key.
Tuesday: Grade 2 printable worksheet + answer key.
Wednesday: Grade 3 printable worksheet + answer key.
Thursday: Grade 4 worksheet + answers.
Friday: Grade 5 scholarship-style reasoning worksheet.
Saturday: Grade 5 explained quiz video (original narration and visuals).
Sunday: Parent/teacher guide and weekly revision roundup.

## Traffic
1. Each paper gets a unique indexed HTML page with the full questions, answer explanation, printable version and canonical URL.
2. Build topic clusters for Sinhala, mathematics, environment and scholarship reasoning.
3. Post useful native Facebook quiz previews without forcing engagement.
4. Submit sitemap after website deployment to Search Console.
5. Track impressions and clicks to discover which grade/topic pages help families.

## Monetization gates
- Confirm public accessible site and sufficient quality original resources.
- Add contact information and meaningful privacy and terms policies before Ads approval application.
- Review child-directed privacy regulations, AdSense policies, and ad request restrictions.
- No ads or ad code before approval. Never purchase fake visits/clicks.
